## Dedicated particles

Fourteen custom particle types replace the vanilla ones across the mod. They are real
`ParticleType` registrations with their own textures and client-side motion, not recoloured
vanilla sprites:

| Particle | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `qi_mote` | Slow rising mote that curls on a sine path and fades; used for meditation, springs and tamed beasts |
| `spirit_flame` | Warm variant used for body tempering and spirit stone release |
| `blood_mote` | Dark red burst when a spirit beast is hurt |
| `qi_ring` / `seal_ring` / `wind_ring` | Ground-anchored expanding rings, camera-facing on the Y axis |
| `qi_spark` / `ember_spark` | Fast tapered sparks that keep momentum and leave a streak |
| `rune_glyph_a` / `rune_glyph_b` | Long-lived rotating rune decals that hang in place and pulse |
| `spirit_smoke` / `alchemy_smoke` | Slow swelling vapour plumes that grow as they fade |
| `spirit_leaf` / `ember_leaf` | Tumbling leaf and feather shards that sway while falling |

## Ritual choreography

`XuanhuanEffects` composes these into named beats: a meditation cycle raises motes, drops a
ring, hangs glyphs and pops sparks; a talisman snaps a seal ring and a rune cluster; a skill
burst differs per cultivation route; alchemy, talisman tables and array cores each have
their own working and completion effects. Settlements, meditation cushions and spirit stones
all use the same vocabulary, so unrelated systems read as one world.

## Creature animation

- Spirit beasts and settlers now expose run speed, hurt time, water state, leap progress,
  tail wag and breathing phase to their models.
- Models react to all of it: hurt head tilt, ear flattening, tucked legs mid-leap, paddling
  in water, faster wing beats when a crane is airborne, and per-role idle fidgets for
  settlers instead of a frozen standing pose.
- Fox and wolf tails are now three-segment chains with independent sway, and both gained
  real ear parts.
- All five spirit beasts have an emissive eye layer, so the eyes stay lit at night.

## Player poses

Cultivation actions are authored in three phases — gather, release, settle — rather than a
single sine loop. Talisman use raises the hand overhead, snaps it down, then drops; body
tempering flares the arms outward; circulation sweeps the hands from chest to side. A light
client-side aura of qi motes or ember sparks follows any player mid-action.
