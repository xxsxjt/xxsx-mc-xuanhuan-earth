## Content pass: alchemy, implements, beast growth, realms

### Alchemy ladder (6 new pills)

Pills were a single item. They are now a ladder where each tier consumes the previous one,
so alchemy reads as progression instead of six unrelated recipes:

| Pill | Materials | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 2 qi pills + 2 crystal shards | +70 mana, regeneration, circulation XP |
| Ironbody | 1 qi pill + 2 geology catalysts | Resistance + absorption, body-tempering XP |
| Cloudbreath | 2 spring bottles + 3 spirit grass | Long water breathing, fetal-breath XP |
| Grainfast | 4 spirit grass + 2 wheat | Saturation, fasting XP |
| Nightsight | 2 spirit grass + golden carrot | Night vision + speed |
| Breakthrough | 2 foundation pills + 4 shards | The only route to a higher realm |

Grainfast and Nightsight also have crafting-table recipes so the ladder has an entry point
before a furnace exists.

### Spirit implements (4 new items)

Spirit iron blanks now make reusable tools rather than only being a talisman ingredient:

- **Wind Fan** — pushes and slows nearby hostile mobs
- **Stone Seal** — resistance and absorption, plus damage to close enemies
- **Gather Orb** — converts stored charge directly into mana
- **Lantern Rod** — night vision and marks nearby creatures

All four hold a persistent charge pool (`implement_charge` data component), charge from the
local qi field on sneak-use, and spend charge per cast. Unlike talismans they are not consumed.

### Beast growth (rank 1-5)

Contracted beasts were static once tamed. They now have a rank separate from affinity:

- Rank rises from feeding spirit crystal shards, and slowly from fighting alongside the owner.
- Rank drives max health (+3/rank), attack (+0.8/rank) and armor (+1.2/rank).
- The beast's name plate shows its rank, so progress is visible without opening anything.
- Rank and XP persist through save and reload.

### Realm breakthrough

Route levels cap at 10 with nothing above them. A realm now sits above the routes:

Mortal → Qi Refining → Foundation → Golden Core → Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation.

- Requires every learned route to reach level 4/6/8/10 respectively, plus a Breakthrough Pill.
- Consumes the pill, refills mana and permanently adds 12 to the xuanhuan mana contribution.
- A **Break Through** button in the cultivation panel reports exactly what is missing.

### Handbook

Three new pages in both languages (Pills, Implements, Realm), so the new systems are
discoverable in game rather than only in the changelog.
